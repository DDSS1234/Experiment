Cellulose Table

2025-Present

The Cellulose Table is a project exploring the use of natural and recycled paper fibers in the creation of structurally stable objects. I'm interested in how this could be utilized in de-monetized making

The removal of currency from construction. Frank Lloyd Wright's "Usonian" homes and Walter Segal's "Segal Method" were the philosophical foundations for this work.

In my hometown of Eagle, WI, we have a museum named "Old World Wisconsin", which has been a major inspiration for this work. The history of the barn showcases cultural and social shifts within the state over time. This can be seen in the early fieldstone foundations, from a period when money had less governance and the farmer required more self-sufficiency. These foundations began to disappear with the advent of cinder blocks, as the dollar’s use ushered in expediency. Heavy timber, and its community barn raisings, disappeared with the rise of light-wood framing.
