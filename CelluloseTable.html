Cellulose Table

2025-Present

The Cellulose Table is a project exploring the use of natural and recycled paper fibers in the creation of structurally stable objects.

The removal of currency from construction. Frank Lloyd Wright's "Usonian" homes and Walter Segal's "Segal Method" were the philosophical foundations for this work.
