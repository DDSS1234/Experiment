Alex Rosno is a designer and artist combining digital tools with physical making in both spaces and objects.

His current work Opus, is focused on developing single cast furniture and architecture from cellulose fibers; allowing either the complete recycling of spatial based goods, or indefinite permanence.

He has a Masters of Science from Sci-Arc where he researched and developed tools and methods to turn cellulose and recycled paper fibers into architectural elements. His thesis examined the integration of house-scale user interfaces and artificial intelligence-based assistance inside of the American home.

All my work is dedicated to my friends and family. None of this would’ve been possible without your love and support.

Originally from Eagle, Wisconsin.
