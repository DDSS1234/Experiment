Alex Rosno is a designer and artist combining digital tools with physical making to create spaces and objects.

His current work, Opus, is focused on developing single cast furniture and architecture from cellulose fibers.

He has a Masters of Science from Sci-Arc, where he researched and developed tools and methods to transform cellulose and recycled paper fibers into architectural elements. His thesis examined the integration of house-scale user interfaces and artificial intelligence-based assistance inside of the American home.

Originally from Eagle, Wisconsin.
