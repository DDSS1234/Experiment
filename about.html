About

Alex Rosno is a Wisconsin designer and artist currently based in Los Angeles.

His current work is combing hand papermaking with digital tools to create scultpural pieces and load bearing paper structures.

He has a Masters in Science from SCI-ARC (2025), an M.Arch in Architecture from Clemson (2024), and a Bachelor of Science in Architecture from UW-Milwaukee (2022).

Open for design commissions and teaching opportunities.
